Privacy Policy

This privacy policy is applicable to all mobile applications, including but not limited to Tango, Mini Sudoku 6x6: Quick Puzzle, One Line Draw: Puzzle Game, and Jigsaw Card Puzzle (hereinafter referred to collectively or individually as the "Application") for mobile devices, developed by Kelvix Studio (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is provided "AS IS".


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Applications do not require users to create an account or register to access their features. The Service Provider does not directly collect personal information through registration forms or user accounts.

However, the Applications use Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service, to display advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information from users' devices to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements, as well as to prevent fraud and ensure advertising security.


What information does the Application collect automatically?

Through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information, including but not limited to:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • Device information, including device type, operating system and settings;
  • Information about interactions with advertisements, including ad views and clicks;
  • Diagnostic information related to advertising services and SDK performance.

The information collected may vary depending on the user's device, advertising settings, consent choices, and applicable laws and regulations. The Service Provider does not operate a separate analytics service to collect user activity within the Applications.


Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

The Applications do not directly request or collect precise real-time location information from users' devices. However, Google AdMob may use the device's IP address to estimate its general location for advertising, security, and fraud prevention purposes.


Does the Application use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies?

The Applications do not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process users' personal data or provide their features.


Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

The Applications use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect, process, and share certain information as described in its own privacy policies and applicable service terms.


For more information about how Google handles information through its advertising services, please consult the following policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and Advertising

Kelvix Studio does not sell users' personal information. Google and its advertising partners may process or share information as necessary to provide advertising services, subject to applicable privacy laws and users' consent choices.


How is advertising consent managed?

The Applications use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage users' advertising consent choices where required by applicable privacy regulations.

Depending on the user's location and applicable requirements, users may be presented with a consent form that allows them to manage their preferences regarding personalized advertising and the processing of their personal data.

Users may also be able to review or change their privacy choices through privacy options provided within the Applications, where available.


What are my opt-out rights?

Users can stop further information collection through the Applications by uninstalling them from their devices. Uninstalling an Application does not automatically delete information that may already have been collected by Google.

Users can also manage advertising preferences and advertising identifiers through their device settings and, where available, through the privacy options provided within the Applications.


What is the data retention policy and how can you manage your information?

The Applications do not require user accounts and the Service Provider does not maintain personal user accounts or registration information.

Information collected through Google AdMob is processed and retained by Google in accordance with its own data retention policies and applicable privacy regulations.

For questions or requests concerning information processed by Google, users can consult Google's Privacy Policy and the privacy controls provided by Google. For any privacy-related questions concerning the Applications, users may contact the Service Provider at kelvixstudio@gmail.com.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 through the Applications. The Applications do not require users to create accounts or provide personal information to access their features.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child under the age of 13 has provided personal information through an Application, they may contact the Service Provider at kelvixstudio@gmail.com.
